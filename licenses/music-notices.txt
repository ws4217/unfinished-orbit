Changes for this game:
All seven music files were converted from the supplied originals to Ogg Opus,
stereo, 96 kbps. Fleeting Colors was additionally sped up by 1.5x, with the
corresponding pitch increase; its runtime playback rate is 1.0.
No new cuts, loop edits or loudness normalization were made during integration.
Music is played whole, with application-level looping and volume fades.
Original author files are preserved outside the runtime package.
